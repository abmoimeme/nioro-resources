Nioro Resources LLC is a United States company founded by Abdoul Samb. It takes its name from Nioro du Rip, in Senegal. As a parent company, it owns and operates businesses that connect American experience with opportunity in Senegal and West Africa. Nioro Resources LLC est une société américaine fondée par Abdoul Samb. Elle tire son nom de Nioro du Rip, au Sénégal.
